Static patches imported from existing original atelier paintings: bow.webp, charm_pearl.webp, pearl_drop.webp, charm_crystal.webp, flower.webp. The satin bow preserves source alpha and painted folds, with a deterministic rose/ivory luminance grade and fine luminance sharpening. The seven-bead pearl strand duplicates the same spherical painted pearl with a short silk connector. Mary Jane ribbon reuses bow satin texture; laces are local supersampled shaded cords with gold eyelets. Runtime adds shoe-contact shadows and a fitted instep band. Rebuild: python3 dev/build-shoe2d-accessories.py (Pillow only). No new AI-generated source is claimed.
